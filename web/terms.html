Terms & Conditions

Draft — version 2026-10-draft. This is a starting template, not legal advice. Have a lawyer licensed in Georgia review it before launch.

1. General (all users)

  1. Eliava ("the platform") connects customers, technicians and sellers. The platform is not a party to repair agreements or sales between users unless stated otherwise.
  2. You must give true information: your real name, a phone number you control and accurate descriptions, photos and videos.
  3. You must not post illegal, stolen, counterfeit or dangerous goods, or content that is offensive or misleading.
  4. We may hide, edit or remove posts and listings, and suspend accounts that break these terms. Fees already paid for a suspended account are not refunded when the suspension is caused by a breach of these terms.
  5. Prices on the platform are in Georgian Lari (GEL) and include VAT where applicable.

2. Customers

  1. Each repair request must describe one real problem, with at least one photo. Posting the same problem more than once is not allowed; duplicates are detected automatically, flagged and hidden from technicians until reviewed.
  2. Your phone number is hidden. A technician sees it only after choosing "View number". You can see which technicians viewed your number, and so can the platform administrators.
  3. Price estimates are automatic guidance only. The final price is agreed directly with the technician before work begins.
  4. Close your request once the problem is solved so technicians stop contacting you.

3. Technicians

  1. To be listed and to view customer numbers you must pay the registration fee for each period. Each "View number" is charged once per request.
  2. You must hold the skills, tools and any licences required for the specialities you select.
  3. Liability for damage: if an appliance, part or property is damaged because of your negligence or faulty work, you must repair it at your own cost or compensate the customer for the damage, up to its fair market value. Disputes are first handled through the platform; we may suspend your account while a claim is open.
  4. You must agree the price with the customer before starting work, and give a receipt on request.
  5. You may not use customer numbers for anything other than offering the requested repair, and may not share them with others.

4. Sellers (market)

  1. To publish listings you must pay the seller registration fee for each period.
  2. Keep prices and the stock quantity of each item accurate. If an order is placed for an item you do not have, you must cancel it promptly.
  3. Select your correct Eliava market stall (e.g. A5, B2) or pin your shop location, so buyers can find you.
  4. Items must match their description and photos. Buyers may return items that are faulty or not as described within 14 days, in line with Georgian consumer protection law.

5. Delivery

  1. When delivery is chosen, the delivery fee is shown before ordering. Delivery is paid on delivery unless online payment is offered.
  2. Check the item on delivery; report damage immediately.

6. Payments, boosts and the leaderboard

  1. Fees are paid from your platform wallet. Registration fees, number views and boosts are not refundable except where required by law.
  2. The leaderboard ranks technicians and sellers by the amount paid for boosts in the current period. Boosted profiles are shown first and are marked as promoted. A boost does not mean the platform endorses the user.

7. Privacy

  1. We store your account details, posts, listings, orders and a log of who viewed which phone number, to run the service, prevent abuse and resolve disputes, in line with the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection.
  2. Hidden data such as GPS location is removed from uploaded photos automatically. Photos and videos you upload are visible to logged-in technicians (repair requests) or to everyone (market listings).
  3. You can delete your account yourself from the Account page. Your name, email, phone, photos and profile are removed, except records we must keep by law (kept without your personal details).
